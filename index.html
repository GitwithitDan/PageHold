How it works

Three steps.
Invisible by design.

01

Intercepts at start

PageHold injects before any content renders — at the very first moment of a page load. Nothing gets painted until it's all ready.

02

Holds the overlay

A clean loading screen covers the page. Images, ads, scripts — everything loads silently behind it while you see a calm progress bar.

03

Reveals all at once

The moment everything is ready, the overlay fades and the fully-loaded page appears in a single clean reveal. No jumps. No chaos.

The problem

Pages used to
stay still.

Modern web pages are assembled from dozens of sources simultaneously — ads, images, fonts, scripts, trackers. Each loads at a different speed, shoving content around as it arrives.

  • You click the wrong thingA button shifts just as your finger lands on it
  • Your reading position jumpsAn ad loads above your text and pushes everything down
  • The layout breaks mid-readImages pop in, columns resize, everything reflows
The solution

One extension.
Complete calm.

PageHold enforces a simple rule: nothing shows until everything is ready. You wait one extra second. In return, you never deal with layout chaos again.

  • Pages land fully-formedEvery element in its final position from the first frame
  • Zero configurationInstall and forget. Works on every site automatically
  • Completely privateNo analytics, no tracking, no data leaves your browser
